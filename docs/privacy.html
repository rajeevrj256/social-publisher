Privacy Policy

Aesthetic Finds Publisher · last updated 19 September 2026

Aesthetic Finds Publisher ("the app") is a private, single-operator tool used by its owner to upload their own videos to their own YouTube channel. It has no other users and offers no service to the public.

Information the app accesses

  • YouTube authorisation. When the owner authorises the app, Google issues it an access token and a refresh token for the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • Channel identifier. The app reads the id and title of the authorising channel so it knows where to upload.
  • Video files and their titles, descriptions and tags, supplied by the owner.

The app does not access viewer data, analytics, comments, subscriber information, or any other person's account.

How the information is used

Solely to upload the owner's videos to the owner's channel via the YouTube Data API v3. The app performs no other operation on the account.

Storage and security

  • OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest and stored in a private database that only the owner can reach.
  • Tokens are never written to logs, never displayed, and never shared.
  • Video files are processed on the owner's own machine.

Sharing

Nothing is sold, rented, or shared with any third party. Data is transmitted only to Google's own APIs in order to perform the upload the owner requested.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the app is in use and are deleted when access is revoked. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections, which immediately invalidates its tokens.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email listed on the app's Google consent screen.
